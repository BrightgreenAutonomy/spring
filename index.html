What we do

Structures that make sense before anyone picks up a tool.

A good build is won early — in the structural logic, the material choices, the junctions, the sequencing and the details that keep site work clean. Spring brings engineering discipline to residential and outdoor construction, so your project is easier to approve, price and build.

  • Fewer variations on siteDetails are resolved on paper, where changes are cheap.
  • Sharper, comparable quotesClear quantities mean builders price the same scope.
  • Faster installationRepeatable modules and prefab components cut build time.
  • Less wasteSpans and member sizes are optimised to standard material lengths.

Ask a question
Do you build the structures as well?

Spring focuses on design, engineering and documentation. We work alongside your chosen builder or fabricator — or can help connect you with trades experienced in the type of structure you're planning.

Is prefab actually faster or cheaper?

Prefab can shorten time on site and reduce waste, especially on remote, tight-access or weather-exposed sites. Whether it's cheaper depends on the project — our Feasibility Check compares the options honestly for your site.

Can your documentation be used for council or certifier approval?

Yes. Our Engineering Pack is prepared to support development and building approval submissions, with drawings and structural information your certifier or council will expect.

What do you need from me to get started?

A site address, a rough description of what you want to build, any sketches, photos or existing drawings, and your timing and budget range. That's enough for us to prepare a fixed-fee proposal.

Do you work outside major cities?

Absolutely. Much of our work suits regional and rural sites, where clear documentation and prefab-ready design make the biggest difference to cost and time.

How are your fees structured?

After a short brief we provide a fixed-fee proposal for the agreed scope, so you know the design and engineering cost up front — no open-ended hourly billing.
